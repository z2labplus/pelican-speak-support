Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

This policy explains how the Grey Tide Operations iOS game handles information.

Information collected by the game

The game does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It has no account, online matchmaking, advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDK. It does not request microphone, camera, or location access. The game is designed for offline play and does not send game activity to the developer.

Information stored on your device

Game preferences, such as language and match options, are saved locally on your device so they remain available when you play again. They are not uploaded to our servers.

The number of free trial matches you have played, and whether today’s free match has been used, is stored in your device’s Keychain so that it is kept if you reinstall the game. This information stays on your device and is not uploaded.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive the address and any information you choose to include, and use it to respond and resolve the request. Please do not include passwords or payment details. You can request deletion of your support correspondence by emailing telegram2ai@gmail.com. We retain correspondence only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations.

Apple services

Apple distributes the app and processes App Store purchases. Apple may independently handle purchase, account, device, or diagnostic information under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. The game does not receive your payment card details.

The game is free to download, with unlimited matches on the first day and 2 free matches a day after that, and offers one in-app purchase, “Unlock Full Game”. The purchase, restoring it, and Family Sharing are handled by Apple’s StoreKit; the game only checks on your device whether the unlock is active and does not send purchase information to the developer.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy if the app’s practices change. The current version will be available at this URL. Questions or privacy requests: telegram2ai@gmail.com.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 4 日

本政策说明《灰潮行动》iOS 游戏如何处理信息。

游戏收集的信息

游戏不会收集、传输、出售或分享个人信息。游戏不提供账号、在线匹配、广告或分析功能，也不集成第三方跟踪 SDK。游戏不会请求麦克风、相机或定位权限。游戏为离线游玩设计，不会向开发者发送游戏活动数据。

保存在设备上的信息

游戏偏好设置（例如语言和对局选项）会保存在你的设备上，以便下次游玩时继续使用；这些设置不会上传到我们的服务器。

你已使用的免费试玩局数、以及当天的免费局是否已用，会保存在设备的钥匙串中，因此重新安装游戏后仍会保留。这些信息只保存在你的设备上，不会上传。

支持邮件

如果你给支持邮箱发邮件，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和你主动提供的信息，并仅用于回复及处理问题。请勿在邮件中发送密码或支付资料。你可以发邮件至 telegram2ai@gmail.com 请求删除支持邮件往来记录。我们只会在处理请求及满足适用法律义务所需期间保留相关邮件。

Apple 服务

应用由 Apple 分发，App Store 购买由 Apple 处理。Apple 可能依据其条款和隐私政策独立处理购买、账号、设备或诊断信息。游戏不会收到你的银行卡信息。

游戏免费下载，第一天不限局数、之后每天免费 2 局，并提供一项名为“解锁完整版”的 App 内购买。购买、恢复购买及家人共享均由 Apple 的 StoreKit 处理；游戏只在你的设备上检查是否已解锁，不会把购买信息发送给开发者。

政策变更与联系方式

若应用的信息处理方式发生变化，我们可能更新本政策；最新版本会继续发布在此网址。隐私问题或请求请联系：telegram2ai@gmail.com。
