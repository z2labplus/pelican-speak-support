How can we help?

If the game will not start, close and reopen it, then try restarting your device. For control or performance issues, include your device model, iOS version, and what happened. Do not send passwords or payment details.

Purchases

The app is purchased through Apple. For a refund or billing issue, contact Apple purchase support; we cannot view or change Apple payment information.

Privacy

The game is designed for offline play. Preferences stay on your device; the app does not use advertising or analytics services.
