Jelly Lab

Support for the calm sliding puzzle with squishy, sunlit jelly for iPhone and iPad. Slide jelly blocks, merge them up to one big peach block, and clear the level. No account, no ads, fully offline.

Email support

How can we help?

If the game will not start, close and reopen it, then try restarting your device. When reporting a problem, include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, the level number and what happened. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

Purchases

The first 20 levels are free. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks all levels, including levels added later. Purchases are handled by Apple. To restore a purchase on a new device, open the level list, tap the gear and choose the restore row, or tap the round arrow on the unlock card. For refunds or billing questions, contact Apple; we cannot see or change Apple payment information.

Privacy

Jelly Lab does not collect any data. Your progress and settings stay on your device; there are no ads, analytics or accounts.
