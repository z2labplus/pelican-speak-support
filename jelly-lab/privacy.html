Jelly Lab · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026. This policy covers the Jelly Lab app for iPhone and iPad.

We collect no data

Jelly Lab does not collect, store on any server, sell or share personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

On your device

Your level progress, research notes and settings (sound, haptics, reduced motion) are saved only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Purchases

The in-app purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment or personal information; Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Offline

The game itself does not use the internet. The only network communication is between your device and Apple when you buy or restore the purchase.

Children

Because no data is collected, the app collects no information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: telegram2ai@gmail.com.

中文

《果冻研究所》隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 27 日。本政策适用于 iPhone 和 iPad 上的《果冻研究所》应用。

不收集任何数据：本应用不收集、不在任何服务器上保存、不出售也不分享个人信息；没有账号、广告、分析或跟踪。

保存在本机：关卡进度、研究笔记和设置（声音、震动、减少动效）只保存在你的设备上，删除应用即一并删除。

购买：App 内购买由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理，开发者不会收到任何付款或个人信息；购买适用 Apple 的隐私政策。

离线：游戏本身不使用网络，唯一的网络通信是购买或恢复购买时你的设备与 Apple 之间的通信。

儿童：由于不收集任何数据，本应用不会收集儿童的信息。

变更与联系：政策如有变更，会在本页发布新版本并更新日期。如有疑问请联系 telegram2ai@gmail.com。
